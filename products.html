Millet Flour

Milled fresh, not left to sit.

Flour loses nutrition the moment it's ground — heat and time both work against it. That's why we mill our millet flour in small, fresh batches rather than stockpiling it for months. Use it for rotis, pancakes, bakes or anywhere you'd reach for wheat flour.
