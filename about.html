Our Story

Grown slow. Kept whole.

Pushti Naturals began with a simple frustration — that the millets our families once relied on had all but disappeared from modern kitchens, replaced by polished grains stripped of everything that made them good for us.

Where We Started

A return to what our kitchens knew.

"Pushti" means nourishment — and it's the word that guided every decision we made. We didn't set out to invent something new; we set out to bring back grains that were quietly disappearing. Finger millet, barnyard millet, foxtail millet, kodo millet and little millet were once everyday staples across South and Southeast Asia, valued for exactly the qualities that get polished away in mass processing.

Today, Pushti Naturals sources and prepares these grains the way they were always meant to be eaten — unpolished, minimally processed, and free of the shortcuts that trade nutrition for shelf appeal. From whole millets to millet vermicelli and stone-milled flour, every product keeps its bran, its fibre and its character intact.

Our Mission

Wellness rooted in tradition.

Everything we make answers to three simple commitments.

Keep It Whole

We leave grains unpolished so the bran, germ and fibre stay exactly where nature put them.

Keep It Honest

No hidden additives, no polishing agents — just the grain, cleaned and packed with care.

Keep It Rooted

We stay close to traditional preparation methods rather than chasing industrial shortcuts.

Looking Ahead

Where we're headed.

We're building Pushti Naturals one honest product at a time — starting with five unpolished millets, a comforting millet vermicelli, and stone-milled flour. As we grow, our promise stays the same: nothing added that doesn't need to be there, and nothing taken away that your body actually needs.
